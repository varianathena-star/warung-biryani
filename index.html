Our Story

From a small kitchen to a table everyone gathers at

Warung Biryani started as a family recipe passed down through generations. We believe good biryani takes time — basmati rice soaked properly, whole spices toasted by hand, then cooked using the dum method: slow-steamed in a sealed pot until every aroma locks in.

Every plate is cooked fresh, never reheated. We deliberately limit how much we make each day so the flavor stays consistent from the first batch to the last.
